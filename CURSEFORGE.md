# MMO Skill Stations Pack

**Milling logs and unmaking gear level you up.**

This pack ties the Sawmill and the Disenchanting Tables from [RPG Stations](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/rpg-stations) into [MMO Skill Tree](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree). Every log you mill pays Woodcutting and Crafting XP, every piece of gear you unmake pays Enchanting and Crafting XP, and two characters in the Forgotten Temple have work for you.

You need a Hytale Update 7 server with RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer, MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 or newer and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.4.0 or newer.

## What it adds

- XP for milling. Each cycle at the Sawmill pays 8 Woodcutting XP and 4 Crafting XP, and a better hatchet multiplies both.
- Finds from every log: Plant Fiber, Tree Bark, Tree Sap and sticks, then Essence of Life and Greater Essence of Life as your levels and luck climb.
- XP for unmaking gear. Each piece you break down at a Disenchanting Table pays 30 Enchanting XP and 15 Crafting XP, and better gear pays more. The higher your Enchanting, the faster the ritual.
- Sawyer Marn and Enchanter Orrin, who stand in the Forgotten Temple. With this pack the Sawmill and the tables can't be crafted: you earn them through their quests.
- 30 quests between the two of them, dailies included, and 58 achievements.
- Three hatchets. The Sawmiller's Hatchet is a rare find at the mill, about 1 in 3,000 cycles, and luck makes it come sooner. The Millwright's Hatchet is yours after milling 150,000 pieces of lumber, and Marn's own ends a short questline of his.
- A woodcutter's kit and a disenchanter's kit, four pieces each, that do more when worn as a set. Orrin's Grimoire speeds up the ritual.
- Pull the Sawmiller's Hatchet or the Disenchanter's Grimoire and you earn a gold crown over your finds. The first player on the server to do it gets a title too.
- 16 contracts for the [MMO Skill Bounty Pack](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree-bounty-board-pack-rich-customizable)'s boards: seven for the mill, nine for the tables.
- Translated into 9 languages.

## How it works

1. Reach the Forgotten Temple and ask the Adventurer's Guide about the one with the hatchet. That's Sawyer Marn.
2. Do his first job, Timber Rights: chop timber, cut planks at a Builder's Workbench and bring him a share. Collect the Sawmill from your quest log and place it.
3. Hold logs and press F to load up to 100. Hold a hatchet and press F again to start milling. Crouch as you press F to pick which cut you want.
4. Walk off, take a hit or run out of logs and the session ends with a summary. Anything left in the bench comes back to you.
5. Ask the guide, or Marn, about the one with the book. That's Enchanter Orrin. Bring him Iron Ingots and Essence of Life and he builds you a Disenchanting Table.
6. Hold a piece of gear and press F to set it on the table, then press F again to start. When it comes apart you get its salvage, Essence of Life and sometimes a find. Orrin's later quests give you the Greater Disenchanting Table, which takes three pieces.

## For server owners

- Everything here is plain content in the zip: find tiers, odds, XP, quests, rewards and both kits. The pack's [README](https://github.com/arfemia/MMOSkillStationsPack/blob/main/README.md#for-server-owners) lists where each one lives.
- Want Marn or Orrin out of the temple? `/mmonpc list` in the temple names both of them, and `/mmonpc disable` takes one away. A pack update won't bring him back.
- Without MMO Skill Tree the stations still run on RPG Stations and pay their own finds, with no skill XP, and the tables can be crafted again. This pack itself needs both mods to load.

# MMO Skill Stations Pack

**Milling logs and unmaking gear level you up.**

RPG Stations gives you a sawmill and two disenchanting tables you can place and work by hand. This pack wires them into [MMO Skill Tree](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree), so every log you mill pays Woodcutting and Crafting XP and every piece of gear you unmake pays Enchanting and Crafting XP. What falls out depends on how far you have come. It also puts a sawyer and an enchanter in the Forgotten Temple with work for you.

You need a Hytale Update 7 server with both mods installed, RPG Stations 1.1.1 and MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1, plus Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer. This pack is just the content between them.

## What it adds

- **XP for milling.** Every completed cycle pays 8 Woodcutting XP and 4 Crafting XP, and a better hatchet multiplies both. Load a stack of logs, press F, and the XP comes in while your character works.
- Offcuts from every log: Plant Fiber, Tree Bark, Tree Sap, sticks. Essence of Life starts turning up at the second find tier, and Greater Essence of Life at the top.
- **Five find tiers**, opened by one score built from your Woodcutting and Crafting luck plus your levels in both. Your own skill counts full, the neighbouring one counts half. The top three tiers also want Woodcutting 30.
- Luck pays out in planks too, not just rare finds. Even a little is worth stacking: you start seeing an extra plank every few cycles, with no far-off threshold to wait on. It stacks with the plank ladder that pays for Woodcutting level at 25, 50, 75, 100 and 125.
- A real chase for the Sawmiller's Hatchet. Around 1 in 3000 cycles with nothing invested, down to about 1 in 762 once your Woodcutting tree is finished. Luck is what shortens it.
- Once you have that hatchet: +10 maximum stamina, +25% Woodcutting XP and +25 Woodcutting luck while held. It also opens a bonus roll nothing else in the game can reach, paying double offcuts and the occasional Woodcutting XP boost. Its own luck bonus feeds the roll it unlocks.
- Sawyer Marn, in the Forgotten Temple, with twelve quests (a daily and a short-cycle repeatable among them) and twenty-six achievements about the mill. Details below.
- **XP for unmaking gear.** Every piece you break down at a Disenchanting Table pays 30 Enchanting XP and 15 Crafting XP, and better gear pays more: double for mithril. Enchanting gets an XP source that needs no enchanting mod.
- A trained enchanter works faster. A ritual takes 34 seconds untrained. Enchanting 100 on its own brings it to about 21, and with the full disenchanter's kit on as well it gets to 15, as fast as it goes.
- Luck sizes the extra Essence of Life each piece gives up. It also makes Essence of the Void, the Voidheart, the four-piece disenchanter's kit and the Disenchanter's Grimoire turn up more often, and the kit and the grimoire grant Enchanting luck themselves.
- Enchanter Orrin, an Outlander priest who left the cult, with eighteen quests and thirty-two achievements about the tables. He hands out both of them, and his own grimoire at the end of his story.
- A second hatchet, the Millwright's, for anyone the trophy never drops for. Mill 150,000 pieces of lumber and it is yours. Cuts the same, wears the same, every bonus a notch smaller.
- A third hatchet, Marn's own, the end of a short questline once you have proven yourself at the mill. A four-piece woodcutter's kit too, earned a piece at a time from Marn's standing trim order. Worn together it is a set: all four give off gold sparks and a bit more Woodcutting luck, and any of the three hatchets in hand on top adds the most.
- Sixteen bounty contracts on the shared bounty board, once you have that installed: seven for the mill, nine for the tables.
- Translated into 9 languages. Anything a translation misses falls back to English.

## Sawyer Marn

Marn is an old Kweebec who saws timber, and he knows how that sounds. He stands in the Forgotten Temple with an iron hatchet in his hand, beside the merchant and across from the Mastery Trainer, and he is the one who hands out sawmills. Once you have made it to the temple, ask the guide about the one with the hatchet, then go and talk to him.

His first job is Timber Rights. Chop timber, cut planks at a Builder's Workbench, bring him a share, and the Sawmill is yours to collect from your quest log. The jobs after it each take one thing the mill does and make you do it properly: First Cut has you run it and bring planks back, Reading the Grain has you crouch at the bench and mill the decorative and ornate cuts, Deep in the Wood asks for the Essence of Life old wood gives up (it wants Woodcutting 20), and A Finer Edge is a long shift at Woodcutting 25. A Second Mill branches off after Reading the Grain and pays a second mill.

Finish A Finer Edge and two more things open up. Trim Work is a standing order that comes round several times a day: the specialty cuts and the offcuts, handed in for a wage that grows with your level and luck, plus tool upkeep, the odd extra find, and the only rolls anywhere for the woodcutter's kit, four leather pieces worth wearing together. Marn's Edge runs beside it, a short questline of its own. Straight Grain has you mill stock for a haft. Cold Metal sends you to a furnace for cobalt while the mill keeps running without you. The Fitting binds the two together, and you walk away with a hatchet of Marn's own pattern.

Standing Order is his other daily, open from much earlier. Planks and bark, delivered to him, once a day on the calendar. What he pays out of the yard grows with your Woodcutting level and luck. Some days there is Essence of Life on top, or a Woodcutting boost token; once in a while a Greater Essence of Life.

Twenty-six achievements sit under a Stations category of their own. Ladders for cycles worked, lumber milled and Essence of Life found. The lumber one is the long haul: nine rungs, five hundred pieces at the bottom and six hundred thousand at the top, and the sixth rung, at a hundred and fifty thousand, hands over the Millwright's Hatchet. One for every wood species put through the mill, one for keeping every offcut, one for a store of Greater Essence of Life. A hidden one for finding a boost token in the shavings. Two for the Sawmiller's Hatchet itself, and one of those is a server first: the whole server hears who pulled the first one out of a mill, and they get the First Sawmiller title. One for keeping Standing Order filled day after day, and a matching one for keeping Trim Work's order filled just as often. One for seeing Marn's Edge through to the hatchet.

Pull that hatchet out of a mill and the Sawmiller achievement pays the Sawmiller's Crown. Gold crown particles over every rare find and every finished session at the bench, for as long as you play there. Cosmetic only, and yours for good.

## Enchanter Orrin

Orrin stands in the Forgotten Temple too, beside Marn, a grimoire in his hand, formal to a fault. He was a priest of the Outlanders' cult until he walked out, and now he draws the void back out of gear at a table. Once you have made it to the temple, ask the guide about the one with the book (or ask Marn about the priest next to him), then go and say hello. Bring Orrin Iron Ingots and Essence of Life and he builds you a Disenchanting Table. With this pack installed neither table can be crafted, so he is where they come from. His next jobs have you work it properly (the later ones want some Enchanting behind you), and the last one hands over the Greater Disenchanting Table: three pieces at once, and it pays more.

His own story is about why he left the cult. It ends with forty pieces unmade at the greater table and a Voidheart from wherever you can find one, and pays Orrin's Grimoire, a working spellbook that speeds the ritual up and gives Enchanting luck while you hold it. Neither table will take it. He keeps three repeatable jobs going, one with its own roll for the disenchanter's kit, and a favour for Marn: unmake a few iron hatchets and carry the ore to the mill. Once he has built you a table he opens his shelf too: a Wooden Earth Wand and a Tribal Blowgun for Essence of Life, the only place to get either. Every Blade, the achievement for unmaking a weapon of every family, wants both. Thirty-two achievements go with his work. Two hidden ones wait on the Disenchanter's Grimoire: one is a server first that pays the First Disenchanter title, and the other pays the Disenchanter's Halo, a gold crown over every find at either table, with sparks and green motes as each piece comes apart.

## How it works

1. Install RPG Stations, MMO Skill Tree, and this pack.
2. Reach the Forgotten Temple and find Sawyer Marn. Chop timber, cut planks at a Builder's Workbench, deliver him a share, and collect the Sawmill itself plus Woodcutting XP from your quest log. Or have an admin give you one. Place it. (With this pack installed the bench has no crafting recipe, so Marn is where it comes from.)
3. Hold logs, press F to load up to 100 into the bench.
4. Press F a second time, holding a hatchet. Your character mills the pile one cycle at a time while you watch. Crouch as you press F to pick which cut you want from the log in the bench.
5. Walk off, take a hit, or run the pile dry and the session ends with a summary. Anything still in the bench comes back to you.
6. Go back to Marn. Every job hands in to him, and each one he settles opens the next.
7. Later, get a Disenchanting Table from Orrin and place it. Hold a piece of gear and press F to set it on the table, then press F once more to start. Watch the ritual: when the piece comes apart you get back what the Salvager's Workbench would, plus Essence of Life and sometimes a find. The greater table takes three, one after another.

## What decides your rewards

| Source | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Your hatchet | Planks per log (one on a starter hatchet, five with the trophy) and the XP each cycle pays |
| Woodcutting + Crafting luck | Which find tier you reach, extra planks per cycle, how fast the trophy chase resolves |
| Woodcutting + Crafting levels | Which find tier you reach, and extra planks per cycle |
| Woodcutting 20 | Opens Deep in the Wood |
| Woodcutting 25 | Opens A Finer Edge, and with it Trim Work and Marn's Edge |
| Woodcutting 30 | Opens the top three find tiers |
| Holding the Sawmiller's Hatchet | Opens a bonus roll only it can reach, plus luck and XP on top |
| Woodcutting level + luck together | What Standing Order and Trim Work pay |
| The piece you unmake | The XP it pays and how much extra essence is on offer. Only good gear gives up void |
| Enchanting + global luck | Extra Essence of Life, the void finds, the kit pieces and the grimoire |
| Enchanting level | How fast a ritual runs, and the odd extra salvage |
| A grimoire in hand | A faster ritual, plus Enchanting luck and XP |
| The woodcutter's kit worn as a set | More Woodcutting luck and XP, and more still with a hatchet in hand |

## For server owners

Everything here is plain content in the zip. You can retune any of it without writing code:

- The sawmill's five find tiers: what it takes to reach each one and what each pays out.
- Both plank ladders, the one for levels and the one for luck.
- The odds on the Sawmiller's Hatchet, and the XP a cycle pays.
- Marn's and Orrin's quests and what they pay. Standing Order and Trim Work pay from tables of their own, so you can change a wage without touching the quest.
- What Orrin's shelf sells, and for how much.
- The achievements and their points.
- All sixteen bounty contracts.
- At the disenchanting tables, the XP a piece pays and how much the Enchanting level speeds up a ritual. The greater table takes its settings from the lesser one until you give it its own.
- The extra essence and salvage a piece gives up. Also the odds on each find: Essence of the Void, the Voidheart, the disenchanter's kit and the Disenchanter's Grimoire.
- The woodcutter's kit as a set: which pieces count and what each tier gives.

Where each of those lives is listed in the pack's [README](https://github.com/arfemia/MMOSkillStationsPack/blob/main/README.md#for-server-owners).

Want Marn or Orrin out of the temple? Leave the pack alone. `/mmonpc list` in the temple names both of them, and `/mmonpc disable` takes one away on the spot. That switch lives in your server's own settings, so a pack update won't bring him back. Their quests hide with the stations feature, so nothing dangles if RPG Stations is switched off.

Without MMO Skill Tree the sawmill and the tables still run on RPG Stations and still pay their own finds, they just grant no skill XP, and the tables go back to being crafted; this pack itself, Marn and Orrin included, needs both mods installed to load at all. Without RPG Stations there are no stations at all.

> **DRAFT** - release-docs pass for the 1.6.0 train. Wording is not final and the entries below are pending a maintainer review pass.

First release: the Sawmill, alongside MMO Skill Tree [[1.6.0]] and the standalone RPG Stations companion mod.

- **The Sawmill.** Press use to pull into a diegetic third-person work loop: a looping sawing animation turns logs into that family's planks one cycle at a time, granting passive Woodcutting and Crafting XP scaled by your held hatchet's power, with luck-tiered bonus loot on a lucky cycle. Sneak and press use to choose which cut you want from the log you are holding: planks, decorative, or ornate.
- **Loaded logs show on the bench.** Load a stack straight into the station and it renders as a real placed prop you can take back with a single press.
- **Session summaries.** Finishing a work session shows a summary of everything made and how much experience each skill earned.
- **Hard dependency on both RPG Stations and MMO Skill Tree.** The station work loop itself (camera, hold, the multi-action step engine, conditional loot) ships in the standalone RPG Stations mod; this pack supplies the Sawmill's block and loot content plus the MMO Skill Tree bridge that turns a work session into skill XP and luck. Without RPG Stations installed, the stations do not exist at all; without MMO Skill Tree installed, the station still works but grants no skill XP.
- **Fully translated.** Station names and interaction hints ship across all 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish).

## Held back for a later release

The Anvil, the Smithing skill, and the Cooking skill are finished but do not ship in 0.1.0, so this release stays a single well-tested station. They are kept intact under `unreleased/` in this pack and come back in one command; see `unreleased/README.md`. Cooking in particular is paired with RPG Stations' own fish-preparation station, which is held back on that side for the same release, so the two return together.

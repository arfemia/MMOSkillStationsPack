Marn's and Orrin's story quests now lead your quest lists as main quests.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]], RPG Stations 1.1.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.4.0 or newer.

### Changes

- The Sawyer's and the Enchanter's story quests show as main quests in your quest lists.

### Fixes

- Fixed Marn's and Orrin's lines in Russian, which now name the Forgotten Temple the way the game does, "храм" in lower case.

The stations pack's Update 7 release. `manifest.json` declares `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` (was `>=0.6.0-pre.13 <0.7.0`) and the dependencies `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`, `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0` and `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree ^1.7.1` (were `>=1.1.0`, `>=2.2.0` and `^1.7.0`). No shipped id changes.

- **Changed: the Millwright's Hatchet moves to rung 6 of the lumber ladder.** `Sawmill_Lumber_T6` (A Town's Worth, `STATION_OUTPUT` `Amount` 150,000) gains `Rewards.Claim`'s first row, `{"Kind": "Item", "Params": {"Item": "MMO_Tool_Hatchet_Millwright", "Count": 1}}`, ahead of its two `Mmo_Xp` rows; `Sawmill_Lumber_T9` (Millwright, 600,000) loses that row and pays its Woodcutting and Crafting XP only. The `$Comment`s of both rungs and of `Server/Item/Items/MMO_Tool_Hatchet_Millwright.json` name rung 6, and the README's and store page's lines say 150,000.
- **Adds the Woodcutter's Kit's buff tile.** Each tier of `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/GearSets/Mmo_Woodcutters_Kit.json` names a `Badge` (ZiggfreedCommon 2.3.0's gear-set tier leaf): `{Pieces 2}` `Woodcutters_Kit_Badge_Pieces`, `{Armor 4, Effect}` `Woodcutters_Kit_Badge_Armor`, `{Armor 4, Held 1}` `Woodcutters_Kit_Badge_Held`. The set shows one tile, the badge of its highest tier that holds. The three effects are new under `Server/Entity/Effects/MMOSkillStationsPack/`, each `Infinite`, `OverlapBehavior` `Ignore`, `Debuff` false, carrying only a `Name` and a vanilla `StatusEffectIcon` (`Armor_Leather_Soft_Hands`, `Armor_Leather_Soft_Chest`, `Tool_Hatchet_Mithril`); the set engine puts them on and takes them off, so nothing else applies these ids. The set's look stays `Woodcutters_Kit_Set`. Names: `gearset.woodcutters_kit.badge_pieces`, `.badge_armor` and `.badge_held` in `mmoskilltree.lang`, in all nine languages.
- The Disenchanter's Kit's matching tile (`RPG_Disenchanters_Kit_Badge_*`, `rpgstations.gearset.rpg_disenchanters_kit.badge_*`) ships in the RPG Stations 1.1.1 jar with its `RPG_Disenchanters_Kit` set; this pack ships no set file for that kit.
- Comments only, no field changed: the sixteen `Bounties/MMOSkillTree/Stations/Bounty_*.json` say their `mmoskilltree:feature` `stations` condition is lifted out of the gate when the contract loads, so the board's draw hides the contract while stations are switched off (they said a board draws without reading `Requires`, so the gate refused at Accept). `Disenchanting_Every_Blade`'s `$Comment` records why Update 7's `Weapon_Bangstick` (Family `Bomb`, refused by the tables) and `Weapon_Flamethrower_Scrap` (no player source) add no criterion; its fifteen criteria are unchanged.
- **Changed: every-skill achievement XP becomes a Skill Lamp.** The 13 achievements whose `Rewards.Claim` paid `Mmo_Xp` with `Skill` `ALL` (`Disenchanter_Server_First`, `Disenchanting_Essence_T3`, `Disenchanting_Master`, `Disenchanting_Rites_Account`, `Disenchanting_Threads_Account`, `Disenchanting_Void_T3`, `Disenchanting_Voidheart_T3`, `Disenchanting_Weekly_Account`, `Sawmill_Essence_T3`, `Sawmill_Master_Sawyer`, `Sawmill_Standing_Account`, `Sawmill_Trim_Account`, `Sawmiller_Server_First`) pay `{"Kind": "Mmo_Lamp", "Params": {"Amount": 3X, "Picks": 3}}` in that row, X being the old amount (2,500 to every skill becomes 7,500 on each of three picks). No `Skills` is authored, so the pool is every skill. The `Mmo_Boost_Token` rows on `Sawmill_Master_Sawyer` and `Disenchanting_Master` and the server firsts' `Title` rows are unchanged, and ids and criterion keys are untouched, so progress carries. `Mmo_Lamp` is MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1's kind, which the manifest's `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` `^1.7.1` already asks for.

The MMO Skill Stations Pack moves to MMO Skill Tree 2.0. The Sawmill and the Disenchanting Tables pay by the talents you take, and their techniques are talents you choose.

Needs MMO Skill Tree 2.0.0, Ziggfreed's CommonLib 3.0.0 and RPG Stations 1.2.0.

## Changes

- The Crafting and Enchanting trees have talents that pay only at the Sawmill or the Disenchanting Tables. What the stations pay, and how fast a ritual runs, follows the talents you take and the gear you wear, where it followed your skill level.
- The Sawmill's finds read your Woodcutting talents and luck, and its collect counts them whatever you're holding. The Disenchanting Tables read your Enchanting Yield.
- The Sawmill's and the tables' techniques are talents now. Take Rip Cut and the Sawmill saws more logs a cycle for you, wearing your hatchet faster. Take Lumberyard and you can load a pile of logs that the Sawmill works on its own. The techniques you hold combine, and a pile you load stays there for everyone.
- Talents at the tables pay in Essence of Life and finds. Unmaking still never gives back a whole extra piece of material.
- The Greater Draw talent can draw Greater Essence of Life out of high-level pieces at the Disenchanting Tables.
- Orrin's Grimoire and the Disenchanter's Grimoire need Arcane Magic 40 to hold, since Magic is five schools now.
- The pack's gear and tools ask for their skill level, hatchets Woodcutting. Gear you carry on your first join after the update keeps working.
- The Adventurer's Guide, now in the Adventurers' Hall in the Temple Hub, still sends you to meet Sawyer Marn and Enchanter Orrin in the Forgotten Temple.

## For server owners

- On a server with the Crafting or Enchanting tree switched off, every station pays its usual payouts.

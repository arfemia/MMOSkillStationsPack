The Millwright's Hatchet comes much sooner, at 150,000 pieces of lumber, down from 600,000.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]], RPG Stations 1.1.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### New

- Added a Woodcutter's Kit icon beside your other effects. Put on two pieces and it shows Woodcutter's Kit: Two Pieces. All four make it Woodcutter's Kit: Full, and holding the Sawmiller's, the Millwright's or Marn's Hatchet as well makes it Woodcutter's Kit: Full, Hatchet in Hand.
- With RPG Stations 1.1.1 the Disenchanter's Kit gets one too. It reads Disenchanter's Kit: Two Pieces, Disenchanter's Kit: Full, or Disenchanter's Kit: Full, Grimoire in Hand while you hold the Disenchanter's Grimoire.

### Changes

- The Millwright's Hatchet is now the reward for A Town's Worth, the sixth rung of the lumber ladder, where it used to wait on the last. Collect it from your achievement book. Millwright, the last rung, still pays its XP.
- Achievements that gave XP to every skill give a Skill Lamp instead. Rub it and choose three skills, and each one gets three times what every skill got before: 2,500 to every skill becomes 7,500 to each of three. That covers the Sawmill's and the tables' server firsts, finishing the Sawmill and unmaking questlines, the top rung of the essence, void and Voidheart ladders, and the achievements for keeping the repeatables going.

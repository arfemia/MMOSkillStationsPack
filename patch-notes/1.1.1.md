This one moves the Skill Stations Pack onto Hytale Update 7. It adds an icon for the Woodcutter's Kit beside your other effects, and the Millwright's Hatchet comes much sooner. It needs an Update 7 server, MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]], RPG Stations 1.1.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### New

- **Added a Woodcutter's Kit icon beside your other effects.** Put on two pieces of the Woodcutter's Kit and one icon shows with your other effects, named for the most the kit is giving you: Woodcutter's Kit: Two Pieces, then Woodcutter's Kit: Full once all four are on, then Woodcutter's Kit: Full, Hatchet in Hand while you also hold the Sawmiller's, the Millwright's or Marn's Hatchet.
- **Added a Disenchanter's Kit icon too, with RPG Stations 1.1.1.** It reads Disenchanter's Kit: Two Pieces, Disenchanter's Kit: Full, or Disenchanter's Kit: Full, Grimoire in Hand while you hold the Disenchanter's Grimoire.

### Changes

- **The Millwright's Hatchet comes at 150,000 pieces of lumber.** It is now the reward for A Town's Worth, the sixth rung of the lumber ladder, where it used to wait on the last rung at 600,000. Collect it from your achievement book. Millwright, the last rung, still pays its XP.

The second station: the Disenchanting Table, in two tiers, and the enchanter who hands them out. Requires MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.0]], RPG Stations 1.1.0 and ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0.

- **The Disenchanting Tables level Enchanting.** Place a piece of gear on a table and a long ritual unmakes it, giving back what the Salvager's Workbench would plus Essence of Life, and now and then a find. With this pack every piece unmade pays 30 Enchanting XP and 15 Crafting XP, and a better piece pays more: iron a quarter more, cobalt to adamantite half again, mithril double, enhanced mithril two and a half times. Worn-out gear pays a little less. Enchanting gets an XP source of its own that needs no enchanting mod.
- **A trained enchanter works faster.** A ritual takes 34 seconds untrained, down to 15. Your Enchanting level shortens it on top of the disenchanter's gear: level 100 on its own brings it to about 21 seconds, and level 100 with the full disenchanter's kit on reaches the 15-second floor.
- **Luck sizes the extra essence.** Enchanting luck and global luck add Essence of Life on top of the table's own wage: up to one more for crude gear, two for iron, three for cobalt to adamantite and four for mithril. The piece decides how rich the band is, and your luck decides how far up it you get. With no luck you get the table's wage and nothing more.
- **Sometimes a little more of the salvage.** At Enchanting 50, again at 100, and once your luck reaches 45, there is a chance of one more of the main material a piece breaks down into. It never adds up to a whole extra unit, so breaking down crafted gear still costs you materials.
- **Luck makes the rare finds likelier.** The more luck you have, the more often Essence of the Void turns up from good gear, and the Voidheart from the best of it. The disenchanter's kit pieces and the Disenchanter's Grimoire come up more often too. The robe stays the rare one.
- **Adds Enchanting luck and Enchanting experience to each disenchanter's kit piece while worn**: 36 luck and 18% experience across all four, so every piece you find makes the next one easier to come by. The Disenchanter's Grimoire gives 20 Enchanting luck and 15% Enchanting experience while held.
- **Enchanter Orrin.** A new character in the Forgotten Temple: an Outlander priest who walked away from the cult, grimoire in hand, formal to a fault. Ask Sawyer Marn about the priest in the corner and he will send you over. With this pack installed you cannot craft either table. Orrin hands them out.
- **Adds the unmaking chain.** The Lesser Table gets you your first table. First Unmaking, Three Kinds of Gear, The Essence Draw, The Scrap Heap, Mithril Unmade and Steady Hands each have you work the table properly, and The Greater Table hands over the Greater Disenchanting Table, which takes three pieces at once and pays more. A Second Table branches off along the way and pays a second one.
- **Adds Orrin's own story.** The Cult's Cloth, A Thread of Void and Outlander Steel walk through why he left and what the Outlanders carry. The Voidheart ends it: forty pieces unmade at the greater table, then a Voidheart from wherever you can find one, and he hands over **Orrin's Grimoire**. It casts like any grimoire, and while you hold it the ritual runs faster and you get Enchanting luck and experience. Neither table will take it, so you can never unmake it by accident.
- **Adds Iron for the Mill.** Marn needs iron. Unmake a few iron hatchets at Orrin's table and carry the ore back to the mill.
- **Adds three repeatables.** Unmaking Rites is a daily. Loose Threads comes round every few hours and pays its own roll for the disenchanter's kit. A Week's Unmaking is the weekly. The first two pay more as your Enchanting level and luck grow.
- **Adds thirty-two achievements under a new Disenchanting heading in the Stations category.** Ladders for pieces unmade (five at the bottom, ten thousand at the top), Essence of Life drawn, Essence of the Void found and Voidhearts. One for a weapon of every family, one for every armor slot, tools, mithril, the greater table and the full kit. Two hidden ones for the Disenchanter's Grimoire. One of them, First Disenchanter, goes to the server's first and is a feat of strength: it counts for no points and pays the First Disenchanter title. One for keeping each repeatable going, one for finishing the unmaking chain and one for finishing Orrin's.
- **Adds Orrin's Shelf.** Once he has built you a table, Orrin sells a Wooden Earth Wand and a Tribal Blowgun for 30 Essence of Life each. Nothing else in the game hands a player either one, and Every Blade wants both unmade. That achievement asks for fifteen weapon families: tribal claws and guns are left out, since no player can bring one to a table.
- **Adds the Disenchanter's Halo.** Pull the Disenchanter's Grimoire out of a table and the achievement pays a flair: a gold crown over every find at either table, sparks and green motes as each piece comes apart, and a burst of void when the void turns up. Cosmetic, and yours for good.
- **Adds nine bounty contracts** for the tables on the shared bounty boards: two on the two-hour board, five daily and two weekly.
- **Adds the Woodcutter's Kit as a set.** Wear two pieces for a little more Woodcutting luck and experience. Wear all four for more, plus some Crafting experience and gold sparks that follow you around. Hold any of the three hatchets (the Sawmiller's, the Millwright's or Marn's) with all four on for the most. The tiers stack. The Novice Cap does not count.
- **The lumber ladder tops out at 600,000 pieces instead of ten million.** Its upper rungs now ask for 60,000, 100,000, 150,000, 250,000, 400,000 and 600,000 pieces of lumber, so the Millwright's Hatchet on the last rung is a long haul you can finish. The first three rungs and every rung's reward stay as they were.
- **A Second Bench is now A Second Mill**, and asks for 1,500 pieces of lumber instead of 10,000 before Marn hands over the second Sawmill.
- **Station contracts pay for the work they ask.** The Long Shift wants 1,500 logs sawn at the Sawmill instead of 300, and its pay drops from 1,000 bounty tokens and 10,000 Woodcutting XP to 750 and 8,000, the same as the two weekly contracts for the tables. Quick Mill and Quick Boards pay 400 Woodcutting XP and no bounty tokens, like every other contract on the two-hour board, and the two new quick contracts for the tables pay 400 Enchanting XP the same way.
- **The Sawmill's essence asks rise by half.** Deep in the Wood wants 60 Essence of Life found and 40 handed in, The Fitting 180, and the contract Essence off the Hatchet 30. Their pay is unchanged. The new work at the tables asks for Essence of Life too: The Essence Draw wants 60 drawn and 30 handed in, A Second Table 45, A Week's Unmaking 150, and the contracts Essence Tithe 150 and Essence in Bulk 450.
- **First Sawmiller is a feat of strength.** Winning the race for the server's first Sawmiller's Hatchet no longer counts toward achievement points, so nobody misses a points milestone for losing it; the winner gets the First Sawmiller title instead.
- **Marn and Orrin call things by the game's own names**: Iron, Cobalt and Thorium Ingots, Essence of Life, Greater Essence of Life, Essence of the Void, Plant Fiber and the Builder's Workbench, so what you are sent to find is what you find.
- **Sawyer Marn sounds like the Kweebec he is.** He always looked like one, and now a few of his lines own it, from his first hello to what he says about the heartwood.
- **Quest steps and achievements read plainly.** A step says "Mill 15 decorative planks" or "Saw 10 logs at the Sawmill" instead of "Collect 15 Decorative from a work station", achievement descriptions say how many they want, and item descriptions put what the item does first and its story underneath. Four Sawmill achievements and one contract are renamed: Something in the Grain is now In the Grain, Sap and Spirit is now Fat of the Log, Board Feet is now The Overflow, Steady Hand is now Sore Arms, and the contract Essence Run is now Essence off the Hatchet. Progress and anything you already earned carry over.
- **Without this pack** the Disenchanting Tables still run on RPG Stations alone and can be crafted there, they just pay no skill XP and nobody hands them out.
- **Fully translated** across all 9 languages: Orrin's every line and his shelf, Marn's new ones, every quest, achievement and bounty, the flair, the set and Orrin's Grimoire.

> **DRAFT** - release-docs pass for the 1.6.0 train. Wording is not final and the entries below are pending a maintainer review pass.

First release: the Sawmill, the Anvil, and two brand-new content-pack skills, alongside MMO Skill Tree [[1.6.0]] and the standalone RPG Stations companion mod.

- **The Sawmill.** Press use to pull into a diegetic third-person work loop: a looping sawing animation turns logs into that family's planks one cycle at a time, granting passive Woodcutting and Crafting XP scaled by your held hatchet's power, with luck-tiered bonus loot on a lucky cycle.
- **The Anvil, and a new Smithing skill.** Two actions at the same station: Convert sharpens a vanilla metal bar, and Enhance runs a hammering ritual that rolls real stat bonuses (defense, crit, lifesteal, and more) onto a placed weapon. Working the Anvil trains **Smithing**, the first skill ever added entirely by a content pack rather than built into the plugin.
- **Cooking, trained at the fish-preparation station.** RPG Stations ships its own fish-preparation station (a cutting board that walks your character to a nearby cooking fire and back); this pack adds a second brand-new skill, **Cooking**, that it trains. See RPG Stations' own docs for the station itself.
- **Session summaries.** Finishing a work session shows a summary of everything made or enhanced and how much experience each skill earned.
- **Hard dependency on both RPG Stations and MMO Skill Tree.** The station work loop itself (camera, hold, the multi-action step engine, conditional loot) ships in the standalone RPG Stations mod; this pack supplies the Anvil, the Sawmill's block and loot content, and the MMO Skill Tree bridge that turns a work session into skill XP, luck, mastery bonuses, and rolled item stats. Without RPG Stations installed, the stations do not exist at all; without MMO Skill Tree installed, the stations still work but grant no skill XP and roll no item stats.
- **Fully translated.** Station names, interaction hints, the sharpened-bar items, and both new skills' names and descriptions ship across all 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish).

First release: the Sawmill, and the sawyer who hands it out. Requires MMO Skill Tree [[1.6.1]] and the standalone RPG Stations companion mod (1.0.0+).

- **The Sawmill.** Press use to pull into a diegetic third-person work loop: a looping sawing animation turns logs into that family's planks one cycle at a time, granting passive Woodcutting and Crafting XP scaled by your held hatchet's power, with luck-tiered bonus loot on a lucky cycle. Sneak and press use to choose which cut you want from the log you are holding: planks, decorative, or ornate.
- **Five find tiers, all the way to the level cap.** One score, built from your Woodcutting and Crafting luck plus your levels in both, decides whether a cycle shakes something extra loose and how rich that something is; the top three tiers also want Woodcutting 30. Each tier wants a higher score than the one below it and pays more life essence when it lands, and the top tier is the only one that never comes up empty. Woodcutting level also pays out on its own, adding an extra plank per cycle from level 25 and climbing to four extra at the cap.
- **The Sawmiller's Hatchet earns its keep.** The sawmill's rare trophy hatchet is more than a faster axe here: while you hold it, it grants +10 maximum stamina, +25% Woodcutting experience, and +25 Woodcutting luck. That luck feeds the find tiers directly, and it opens a bonus roll no other hatchet in the game qualifies for, worth another plank on the cycles it lands - so the hatchet's own luck improves the odds of the reward only the hatchet unlocks.
- **Loaded logs show on the bench.** Load a stack straight into the station and it renders as a real placed prop you can take back with a single press.
- **Session summaries.** Finishing a work session shows a summary of everything made and how much experience each skill earned.
- **Sawyer Marn.** A new character in the Forgotten Temple, hatchet in hand, beside the merchant and across from the Mastery Trainer. He is the one who hands out sawmills, and every job about the mill is his: he offers it, you hand in to him, and each one he settles opens the next. Once you have reached the temple, the Adventurer's Guide can point you at him.
- **Adds the Timber Rights quest.** Marn's first job: chop timber, cut planks on a hand bench, and deliver him a share, then collect the Sawmill itself plus Woodcutting experience from your quest log.
- **Adds a chain of Sawmill jobs.** First Cut has you run the mill and bring planks back. Reading the Grain has you crouch at the bench and mill the decorative and ornate cuts. Deep in the Wood asks for the Life Essence old wood gives up, and wants Woodcutting 20. A Finer Edge is a long shift, wanting Woodcutting 25, and pays a Woodcutter's Novice Cap alongside Life Essence and experience. A Second Bench branches off after Reading the Grain and pays a second mill. Between them they pay hatchets, Woodcutting boost tokens, essence and experience.
- **Adds Standing Order, a daily.** Planks and bark, delivered to Marn, once a day on the calendar. What he pays out grows with your Woodcutting level and luck, and some days there is Life Essence, a Woodcutting boost token or a Concentrated Life Essence on top. It counts as a daily wherever the game counts those.
- **Adds Marn's Edge, a short questline for anyone who has finished the sawmill chain.** Straight Grain has you mill plain stock for a haft. Cold Metal sends you to a furnace for cobalt while the mill keeps running without you. The Fitting binds the two together into a hatchet of Marn's own pattern, tempered in Life Essence and yours to keep.
- **Adds Trim Work, a standing trim order that comes round several times a day.** Open once A Finer Edge is done, it pays out alongside Standing Order rather than in place of it: the decorative and ornate cuts, plus offcuts, handed in for a wage, upkeep, the odd find and the only rolls anywhere for the woodcutter's kit.
- **Your Woodcutting level decides which wage you are on; your luck decides how a run goes.** The level you have reached puts you on one of four wage tiers, and luck decides whether a run at that tier pays the lean end of it or the rich end. Two woodcutters at the same level with different luck take home visibly different hauls, and no amount of luck reaches a tier you have not earned.
- **Luck makes the rare things likelier rather than bigger.** Concentrated Life Essence, a boost token and every piece of the woodcutter's kit come up more often the more luck you have invested, to a ceiling. The kit grants Woodcutting luck itself, so each piece you find makes the next a little easier to come by. Global luck counts everywhere Woodcutting luck does.
- **A trim order pays offcuts, saplings and sometimes light.** Fibre, bark, sap and sticks come off the saw, the yard hands back saplings for the trees that fed the mill, and a well-paid order throws in a torch or a lantern for the yard.
- **The yard keeps you in tools you can still use.** Below the top of the trade a spare Copper, Iron or Thorium hatchet is a real upgrade, and one turns up now and then. Past that point it pays repair kits instead, since anybody that far along already carries something better than a spare.
- **Adds seven bounty contracts** for the shared bounty board, once you have that installed too: two on the two-hour board, four daily, one weekly, all paying bounty tokens and Woodcutting experience for milling, working the bench or delivering planks.
- **Adds twenty-six achievements under a Stations category of their own.** Ladders for cycles worked, lumber milled and Life Essence found; one for every wood species put through the mill, one for keeping every offcut, one for a pouch of Concentrated Life Essence; a hidden one for finding a boost token in the shavings; Standing Account for keeping Standing Order filled day after day and Trim Account for doing the same with Trim Work, Master Sawyer for finishing the sawmill chain and Marn's Own for finishing Marn's Edge. Two are for the Sawmiller's Hatchet itself, both hidden until they happen, and one of those is a server first that the whole server hears about.
- **The lumber ladder runs nine rungs, from five hundred pieces to ten million.** It is the long haul of the pack, and the last rung is the only achievement here that hands over an item: the **Millwright's Hatchet**, for anyone the mill never dropped the trophy for. It cuts and wears exactly like the Sawmiller's Hatchet and every bonus sits a notch under it: +5 maximum stamina, +22% Woodcutting experience and +22 Woodcutting luck, plus +12% Crafting experience the trophy does not carry. Onyxium head, so nobody confuses the two, and no crown comes with it.
- **Adds the Sawmiller's Crown.** Pull the trophy hatchet out of a mill and the Sawmiller achievement pays a cosmetic flair: gold crown particles over every rare find and every finished session at the bench, kept for good.
- **Hard dependency on both RPG Stations and MMO Skill Tree.** The station work loop itself (camera, hold, the multi-action step engine, conditional loot) ships in the standalone RPG Stations mod; this pack supplies the Sawmill's block and loot content, the sawyer and his work, plus the MMO Skill Tree bridge that turns a work session into skill XP and luck. Without RPG Stations installed, the stations do not exist at all; without MMO Skill Tree installed, the station still works but grants no skill XP, and there is nobody to give Marn's work to.
- **Fully translated.** Station names and interaction hints, Marn's every line, and every quest and achievement ship across all 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish).

## Held back for a later release

The Anvil, the Smithing skill, and the Cooking skill are finished but do not ship in 1.0.0, so this release stays a single well-tested station. They are kept intact under `unreleased/` in this pack and come back in one command; see `unreleased/README.md`. Cooking in particular is paired with RPG Stations' own fish-preparation station, which is held back on that side for the same release, so the two return together.
